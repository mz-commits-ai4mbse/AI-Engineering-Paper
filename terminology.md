## Writing Rules

- Do not replace controlled terms with stylistic synonyms merely for variation.
- Human Engineering Authority and Model Authority describe concrete authority roles from
  the investigated architecture.
- Human Authority may be used as the generalized concept.
- Internal Engineering Model (IEM) is the concrete implementation-specific state.
- controlled engineering state is the generalized function.
- Project Fit is implementation-specific and should not be presented as a universal
  engineering mechanism.
- Consensus does not imply correctness.
- Transferability beyond the investigated SysML v2 task is a hypothesis, not an empirically
  validated result.
